Who We Are

Engineering Excellence
Redefined

Founded in 2016, Yeti Racing is the official Formula Student team of the School of Engineering at Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT). With 50 driven members, we design and build high-performance Formula race cars to compete at the highest level.

At Formula Bharat 2025, held at Kari Motor Speedway, Coimbatore, we made history by becoming the first team from Kerala to win the championship title in the combustion category.
